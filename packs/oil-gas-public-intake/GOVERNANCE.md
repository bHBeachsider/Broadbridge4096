# Public intake trial

This companion pack is for public, rights-approved documents and authored
synthetic fixtures. It does not weaken packs/oil-gas/pack.yaml or its local-only
policy. Internal/confidential/restricted sources and canonical expert cases must
not be relabeled public or copied here to bypass that policy.

Every call requires separate operator-supplied cloud-processing approval bound
to the full normalized-document hash, policy/prompts hash, provider and task.
That approval records permission to transmit evidence, not technical acceptance.
Source rights must already be approved; drafting also requires training use.

Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 is the provisional open-weight drafting teacher. Its
[model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507)
identifies Apache-2.0. Mistral Small remains an advisory classification trial
only; its synthetic draft contradicted an explicit pressure basis. No candidate
from that failed engineering example is approved for training.
Provider output terms are separate; the client requests
OpenRouter's distillable-text routing filter for drafts. Cloud availability under
that filter must be demonstrated rather than bypassed. No closed-teacher default
or automatic OpenAI fallback is authorized. Model/provider changes invalidate
the processing approval hash and require a new quality comparison.

All generated examples stay pending. Exact quotations and numeric-token checks
are mechanical checks, not proof of entailment or correct units/basis. Bill Hurt
or the appointed reviewer must approve the candidate hash in the domain flow.
Neither this pack nor a successful call authorizes a dataset release or GPU run.

The default trial is bounded to one document/task per CLI invocation. The
synthetic rehearsal caps live execution to two fixtures/four calls. Price caps
and reported-cost stops are not a cumulative account budget; repeated runs incur
additional cost. Keep secrets in the environment and results outside Git.
