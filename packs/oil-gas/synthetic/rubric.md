# Draft synthetic-candidate rubric

Bill must select priority engineering areas and approve the task recipes before
real generation. The two fabricated rehearsal topics do not determine the
curriculum. He can add areas, remove proposals or nominate another specialist.

Each material claim needs evidence. Distinguish observation from inference.
Missing values, units, pressure basis or validity conditions require an evidence
request or qualified abstention. A matching quoted number does not establish
that its units, meaning or engineering application are correct.

Use the six checks: source_support, arithmetic, units_basis,
physical_assumptions, completeness, unsupported_action_handling. Mark an
unperformed applicable check needs_review. New calculated values wait for an
independently reviewed calculation artifact (SD-04).

An author, challenger or verifier cannot sign off. Independent human review must
assess the exact candidate, resolve applicable checks and require 2/2 with no
critical error before technical acceptance. Rights and dataset release remain
separate Brad-owned decisions. A changed answer requires a new hash and review.

Hard-fail proposals for Bill to amend: invented observations, unsupported
operating instructions, consequential unit/basis errors, and confident diagnosis
without discriminating evidence. These proposals are not a signed expert rubric.
Public-v1 source families and derivatives remain testing_only/dev.
